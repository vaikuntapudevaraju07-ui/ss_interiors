Systematic Execution

Our 4-Stage Architectural Process

A seamless roadmap built to eliminate cost creep and schedule delays.

1
Discovery & Site Audit Week 01

Laser spatial measurement, structural checks, lifestyle intake, and budget baseline alignment.

2
3D Visualization & BOQ Week 02-03

Hyper-realistic VR renders, lighting plans, physical material moodboards, and transparent itemized quote.

3
In-House Fabrication Week 04-06

Off-site factory CNC cutting, edge banding, and modular joinery assembly under strict humidity controls.

4
On-Site Turnkey Handover Week 07-10

Swift on-site installation, zero mess assembly, MEP testing, professional deep clean, and white glove handover.
