Studio Narrative

Where Visionary Architecture Meets Meticulous Craftsmanship

Founded in Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh, SS Interiors & Infrastructures has grown from a boutique architectural consultancy into one of the region’s premier turnkey interior design and infrastructure firms. We believe every space tells a personal story. Our multidisciplinary team of licensed architects, interior designers, civil engineers, and master woodworkers collaborate seamlessly to transform bare concrete into warm, breathtaking homes and inspiring commercial headquarters.

Rigor & Sequence

Our 6-Step Working Process

A seamless turnkey journey engineered to eliminate stress and guarantee timely delivery.

01

Consultation

Initial in-depth dialogue exploring your functional routines, aesthetic desires, family dynamics, and budget contours.

02

Site Visit & Measurement

Laser-guided 3D spatial scanning of your site to catalog exact structural beams, conduit lines, natural sun paths, and masonry dimensions.

03

Design & Planning (3D Renders & VR)

Comprehensive 2D spatial layouts followed by photorealistic 3D renders and interactive VR walkthroughs with full lighting simulations.

04

Material Selection

Guided tactile curation at our Rajahmundry studio: feel solid woods, veneer finishes, custom quartz slabs, and hardware tactile response.

05

Turnkey Execution & Fitout

Precision off-site modular assembly followed by swift, clean on-site installation overseen by qualified civil engineers and site leads.

06

Snag-free Handover

120-point quality audit, deep professional cleaning, handover documentation, and certificate of 10-year warranty.
