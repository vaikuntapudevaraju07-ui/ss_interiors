Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide 3D visual designs before starting work? expand_more
Yes. Our studio provides high-definition photorealistic 3D renders, materials moodboards, and precise 2D MEP layout drawings before any procurement or on-site craftsmanship commences.
What is your typical turnaround & handover timeline? expand_more
Standard residential 3BHK interiors and modular systems are handed over in 45 to 60 working days from design sign-off. Bespoke luxury villas and commercial structures follow milestone phases agreed during initial scope finalization.
Do you handle civil and electrical work along with interiors? expand_more
Absolutely. SS Interiors & Infrastructures is a licensed turnkey infrastructure specialist. We orchestrate complete civil alterations, plumbing, electrical redesign, false ceilings, flooring, HVAC, and final painting in-house.
